How it works

Four steps between you and a lab-grade result.

01
🧪

Collect

Catch a mid-stream sample in the cup provided in your kit. No lab visit needed.

02
💧

Dip the strip

Submerge the strip for two seconds, then set it flat on a clean, dry surface.

03
📷

Scan

Frame the strip in the app. Verascan reads all ten pads at once, in real light.

04
📄

Get your result

A lab-grade readout in under 15 seconds — ready to save or send to your clinician.

Inside the scan

Built to correct for the light in your bathroom, not a lab.

Phone cameras, tungsten bulbs, shadows — every scan looks different. Verascan's model normalizes color against the strip's own reference pads before it ever reads a result, so lighting stops being a variable.

  • ✓Auto white-balance correction against the strip casing
  • ✓Alignment guide confirms all 10 pads are in frame
  • ✓Results discarded from device after sync — nothing lingers
